# Data Sources

StickerLog currently includes only structural checklist data:

- album slot count
- team codes
- sticker numbering pattern
- product/package templates

No official sticker images, logos, player portraits, or copied album artwork are included.

## Official Sources

- Panini pack contents page: 980 stickers, 48 teams, 20 stickers per team, numbering pattern  
  https://www.paninigroup.com/en/wc26pack-contents

## Notes

Community trackers and Reddit posts may be used for cross-checking, but data is not copied into this repository unless the source has a clear compatible license.

Player names are intentionally omitted until a clearly licensed or authoritative source is available.
